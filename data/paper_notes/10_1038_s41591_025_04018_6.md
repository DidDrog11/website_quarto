Zoonotic diseases spill over unpredictably, and that makes vaccine trials hard to run. A trial needs enough cases at its sites to show whether the vaccine works, and for Lassa fever nobody can say in advance where those cases will be.

This commentary in *Nature Medicine* proposes a One Health Trial Design. Ecological and social data enter the pipeline at three points. Viral diversity across the reservoir's range informs what the vaccine has to cover. Models of where the reservoir lives, and where people meet it, guide the choice of trial sites. Data on rodent contact and household exposure sharpen the analysis of how well the vaccine performs.

Lassa fever is the test case. The same design applies to any pathogen that arrives from animals, which is most of those on the priority lists for pandemic preparedness.
