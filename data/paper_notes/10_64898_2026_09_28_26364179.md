The 2026 Andes virus outbreak aboard the cruise ship MV Hondius caused 11 confirmed cases and 3 deaths, and the place of infection was disputed, with media attention on a landfill in Tierra del Fuego.

This preprint evaluates the competing exposure hypotheses with open data alone: occurrence records of the principal reservoir, *Oligoryzomys longicaudatus*, rodent serosurveys, the locations of historical Andes virus outbreaks, and the index case's own publicly posted record of their movements.

Historical outbreaks with person-to-person transmission concentrate in southern Chile and adjacent western Argentina, where the reservoir occurs and where several ecological and genetic models place elevated risk. The index case was documented within this transmission focus shortly before the putative exposure period. The open-data approach rapidly located the probable initial exposure within the established focus and set out scenarios to prioritise further epidemiological investigation.
